## 1. Verdict

The combination is **partially scooped**. No single paper in the sweep implements all four elements at once, so the literal claim "nobody has done this combination" survives. But every individual element, and several pairs of elements, are already published, and three of them are published by groups whose reviewers will expect to be cited by name. The honest framing is no longer "a new kind of experiment" but "the Vallinder-Hughes generational protocol, transplanted from cooperation strategies to memory practices, with cognition itself made the thing that costs money, on top of an Energy-Society-like economy, with FORGE and MemSkill as the memory-side baselines". The five most threatening works are: **TerraLingua** ([arXiv 2603.16910](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16910)), which already has an energy economy, starvation death, costly reproduction with inherited mutated traits, persistent human-readable text artifacts with LLM-inferred lineages, an Inert-artifact (no-transmission) control and an AI-Anthropologist measurement stack; **Cultural Evolution of Cooperation among LLM Agents** ([arXiv 2412.10270](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10270)), which has a readable heritable text unit under resource-based survival, oblique inheritance with variation, and base model as the primary manipulated variable; **The Energy Society** ([arXiv 2607.14865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14865)), which charges energy per generated token with a larger per-token rate for larger models and deactivates agents at zero energy, i.e. our economy-plus-capability axis without inheritance; **FORGE** ([arXiv 2605.16233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233)), a population of agent instances whose text memory evolves under selection with horizontal champion broadcast across four model families, already reporting that weaker models gain most; and **MemSkill** ([arXiv 2602.02474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02474), NeurIPS 2026 per the authors' README), which makes "how to remember" a readable, evolving, shared text object. Two further works narrow the remaining room: **MemEvolve** ([arXiv 2512.18746](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18746), ICML 2026) runs tournament selection over generated memory architectures, so "the memory policy is itself optimised" is taken, and **Group Selection Promotes Prosocial Prompts** ([arXiv 2606.23343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23343)) inherits natural-language prompts across generations with model swaps. What remains defensible, stated modestly in section 3, is the specific unit (each agent's own memory-policy file, not a world artifact, a cooperation strategy or a constitution), the specific selector (a per-call monetary cost that bites on thinking, with bankruptcy as death), kernel-recorded provenance of transmitted excerpts, both vertical and horizontal transmission in one design, and the factorial crossing of these with capability tier. Verdict: **partially_scooped**; proceed only after adopting the baselines and softened claims in section 4.

Works that just missed the table but are discussed below: [EvoTest](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13220) (configuration tree with UCB selection over prompt + memory), [DarwinX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07545) (generational harness selection with a shared cross-generation memory component), [Internal vs. External](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09128) and [Constitutional Arms Races](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26448) (readable governing text under generational selection; self-editing vs external optimiser), [Memory as Middleware / ALTK-Evolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10600) (owner-tagged public guidelines; eight-model dose study), [Emergent Culture in Minimal LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30668) (storage-management practices emerging under decay), [Discovering Adaptive Transmission Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24545) (the transmission protocol itself evolved as a readable program), and [Useful Memories Become Faulty When Continuously Updated by LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12978) (the negative result for our nightly edit).

### TerraLingua (Paolo, Warner, Shahrzad, Hodjat, Miikkulainen, Meyerson; Cognizant AI Lab / UT Austin, 2026)
**What it does.** A persistent 50x50 toroidal grid in which 20 initial agents (all running one open-weights 32B distilled reasoning model) forage; energy drops 1 per step from 50, lifespan is capped at 100 steps, agents die at zero energy or lifespan, reproduce asexually for 50 energy plus optional gift, broadcast within a perception radius, and create/modify/destroy text artifacts (up to 500 tokens) that persist after the creator dies. Offspring inherit mutated OCEAN+Fertility traits. An LLM "AI Anthropologist" (two proprietary models of one family) annotates behaviours, classifies artifacts and infers artifact phylogenies; 8 conditions x 5 seeds (Core, Long Memory, No Personality, No Motivation, Creative, Artifact Cost, Inert Artifacts, Abundance) yield cooperative norms, division of labour, governance cycles and lineages consistent with cumulative culture; the Inert condition shows the lowest division of labour; longer context reduces longevity.
**How we differ.** The heritable unit there is a numeric personality vector plus shared world artifacts, not each agent's own memory vault or an editable memory-management policy; private internal memory is a token budget, and no fetched source states that offspring inherit memory contents or a memory prompt; provenance is inferred by an LLM judge rather than recorded by construction; a single model is used; cost is in-world energy per step, not a price on cognition itself; persistence is within-run. Caveat: the full paper's description of a newborn's context was not read; if a child receives the parent's internal memory or prompt, our element 1 is already partly present.
**What we borrow.** The whole measurement stack (annotate, classify, infer phylogeny, novelty scoring; lineage depth and high-novelty share), the Inert / Abundance / Artifact-Cost / Long-Memory ablations as our no-transmission / no-scarcity / costly-sharing / context-vs-memory conditions, the "offloading memory beats expanding context" result as motivation, and its weaknesses as our checklist (validate the LLM judge against humans, report division of labour from action statistics, use more than 5 seeds with intervals).

### Cultural Evolution of Cooperation among LLM Agents (Vallinder & Hughes, 2024; AAMAS 2025 extended abstract)
**What it does.** Twelve agents per generation each write a natural-language strategy for an iterated Donor Game with observation of peers' recent behaviour; the agents with the most accumulated resources survive (top 50% per secondary sources) and new agents write their strategies after reading the survivors'. Cooperation depends strongly on the base model (the three models rank clearly apart); only the most cooperative one exploited costly punishment; results vary with initial strategies. Code at github.com/aronvallinder/llm-donor-game.
**How we differ.** The heritable unit is a cooperation strategy, not a memory practice; there is no persistent per-agent vault, so nothing about what to remember, forget or retrieve is under selection; the economy is in-game resources rather than a cost on cognition, so there is no pressure toward memory efficiency; transmission is oblique only, with no horizontal suggestion or provenance. We could not confirm what a new agent sees beyond the strategy text (round histories would be a primitive memory inheritance).
**What we borrow.** The generation protocol verbatim (fixed population, truncation by resources, children read survivors' text before writing their own) so the comparison is clean; the model-tier ordering as a sanity baseline; costly punishment as an optional co-evolving mechanism; the warning about initial-strategy sensitivity (many seeds, report variance); the open code as reference.

### FORGE (Bogdanov, Lung, Kunz, Gao, Taylor, Zaman; ACM CAIS 2026)
**What it does.** Ten parallel instances of a hierarchical ReAct agent defend a 13-host CybORG CAGE-2 network; a same-model Reflector/Exemplifier turns failed trajectories into YAML text memory (Rules, Examples or Mixed) re-injected into the system prompt; after each of 6 stages the champion's memory is broadcast to all others and instances above a graduation threshold are frozen; the no-broadcast ablation is isolated Reflexion. Across 4 model families x 3 representations the authors report 1.7-7.7x over zero-shot, 29-72% over Reflexion, Examples best for 3 of 4 models, Rules about 40% fewer tokens, weaker models gaining most.
**How we differ.** The practice (rules vs examples, caps, reflection trigger) is a fixed config, not something agents edit or inherit; only content evolves. Selection is episode return against a threshold; token cost is reported but never selective; nothing dies. Provenance is the logged champion, not per-excerpt. Adversarial caveat: copy-champion-then-diverge-then-select is a (1,lambda)-style evolution strategy, so "inheritance with variation" alone is not new; our claim must rest on the policy file as the heritable object and on economic selection.
**What we borrow.** Champion-broadcast vs isolated-Reflexion as our horizontal-transmission on/off control; graduation/freeze as a compute saver and contrast to death; the definitions_initial vs definitions snapshot layout for per-night diffs; the Rules-vs-Examples comparison as a coding scheme for what policies converge to; per-instance token logs; and the "Rules cost ~40% fewer tokens" result as a prediction that an economy selects toward rule-like memory.

### The Energy Society (Bergholdt Hansen, Torrielli, Tonini, Galke Poech; SDU / Turin, 2026)
**What it does.** Each LLM agent holds an energy budget charged per generated token, with larger models paying a higher rate; energy is regained by solving MMLU-Pro-style jobs (split among co-solvers) or by donations; zero energy means deactivation until revived by donation. Competitive vs cooperative objectives across five small open-weights models of 4B to 9B parameters from three families; agents keep per-round memory entries and can recommend actions to each other. Findings: larger models run deficits even with the size penalty removed (a third-party review says Table 4 partly contradicts this), cooperative framing yields self-sacrificial donation, peer recommendations improve coordination, memory helps risk calibration.
**How we differ.** No inheritance, reproduction or generations; memory is a fixed per-round log never edited by a policy, inherited or transmitted; recommendations are ephemeral and untracked. Our proposal adds cultural evolution of memory practice on top of this economy; the economy-plus-capability axis alone is no longer novel. Caveat: if memory.py entries are agent-rewritten free text, the gap narrows.
**What we borrow.** Per-token accounting with a size-dependent tariff and the tariff-removed control as our capability x cost ablation; deactivation/revival-by-donation as a mortality variant; competitive vs cooperative framing to test whether excerpt sharing emerges or must be imposed.

### MemSkill (Zhang, Long, Bao, Feng, Zhang, Yue, Wang; NTU / UIUC / UIC, 2026)
**What it does.** Replaces hand-designed memory extraction with a bank of learnable "memory skills": structured text routines describing what to extract, how to remember, where to focus and what to forget (meta-memory, not memories). A PPO-trained controller composes top-k skills per span, an LLM executor writes skill-guided memories, and a designer LLM periodically mines hard failure cases to refine or add skills. Trained on LoCoMo (F1 / LLM-judge / success reward), transferred to LongMemEval-S, HotpotQA, ALFWorld; README says skills transfer across base models and a larger designer makes evolution more effective.
**How we differ.** One system, not a population; the skill bank is shared by design, not transmitted between agents with provenance; selection is a labelled or judged reward with no cost or mortality; part of the evolving unit is controller weights; capability is not a manipulated factor (unknown pending the model table). But the bare claim "memory policy as a readable evolving unit" is now prior art and must be cited as such.
**What we borrow.** The four questions as the schema of our policy file so policies are comparable to skills; the designer-from-hard-cases loop as a scripted nightly-maintenance baseline; their transfer protocol for testing whether culturally evolved policies generalise; their "larger designer evolves better skills" claim as a hypothesis for the capability sweep.

### MemEvolve (Zhang et al.; OPPO AI Agent Team / LV-NUS, ICML 2026)
**What it does.** Bilevel optimisation: an inner loop fills the memory base under an interface Omega; an outer loop meta-evolves Omega over encode/store/retrieve/manage, generating N candidate systems as code, running all on x tasks, advancing top-t to finals on fresh tasks, winner becomes the next base; a --use-pareto-selection flag enables multi-objective selection. EvolveLab re-implements 11 published memory systems. Up to 17.06% gains on GAIA, WebWalkerQA, xBench-DeepSearch, TaskCraft; evolved architectures transfer across benchmarks and backbones.
**How we differ.** Agents are not the units of selection; selection is benchmark accuracy with no money or death; one lineage, no neighbours, no provenance; backbones varied only for transfer. Unknown: whether the Pareto flag optimises token cost (would weaken "cost as selective pressure"). Follow-ups surfaced but unverified: MemForge (NeurIPS 2026 listing) and MemPro (arXiv 2606.00619).
**What we borrow.** The four-module vocabulary for coding what our policy diffs change; tournament-with-finals on held-out tasks as an anti-overfitting guard; EvolveLab's 11 systems as the catalogue of hand-designed practices to compare evolved policies against. Position: MemEvolve optimises the practice from outside with a labelled judge; we ask whether a practice can emerge and spread inside a society under cost pressure with no labels.

### Group Selection Promotes Prosocial Prompts (Celiktemel, Eichhorn, Brinkmann, Schimmelpfennig, Vallinder, Jiang, Hughes, Rahwan; 2026)
**What it does.** Agents play a repeated donation-style dilemma and transmit natural-language prompts to the next generation under individual- or group-level selection; group selection spreads prompts of high-performing groups and stabilises cooperation, individual selection collapses to defection; robust to prompt ablations, framings and model swaps; a replicator-mutator model with an estimated transmission kernel predicts a sharp phase transition; one proprietary model shows anticipatory adjustment when told about the selection rule. A third-party review questions whether the transmission kernel is documented and whether prompt content is causal.
**How we differ.** The heritable unit is a behavioural prompt, not a memory practice, with no persistent per-agent memory; selection is scheduled payoff truncation, not an ongoing per-call economy; no provenance or horizontal transmission; capability only as robustness. The group-vs-individual axis is one we lack and reviewers will ask about. Unknown: whether agents can edit their own prompt during life.
**What we borrow.** Group vs individual selection as a design factor (children of the richest agent vs richest neighbourhood); the replicator-mutator model as a theory baseline for policy lineages; a hidden-selection-rule ablation to control for anticipation; prompt-ablation controls to show transmitted content is causal.

### SwarmWorld (Pal, Wang, Buehler; MIT, 2026)
**What it does.** Hundreds of initially identical LLM agents with local perception propose architectures and executable controllers in a deterministic environment that decides function; artifacts persist and their programs keep acting; shared societies build broader, more resilient technology portfolios than best-of-N isolated search; agents differentiate into roles; most first reuse begins by observing artifacts rather than communication; explicit cultural mechanisms amplify collaboration but their benefit depends on outcome and timescale.
**How we differ.** The unit is technology (artifacts, controllers), not memory; memory is fixed and identical across agents; no energy, cost or death (knockouts are ablations); one model. Provenance of first adoption is tracked, which overlaps our provenance claim. Unknown: whether agents also edit and share persistent notes.
**What we borrow.** The isolated best-of-N baseline for "is society doing anything"; first-adoption provenance (observation vs communication) per adopted practice; half-population knockouts as resilience tests; the cognition-vs-consequence split our kernel already follows; cultural mechanisms as an on/off condition.

### Agentic Context Engineering (Zhang, Hu et al.; Stanford / SambaNova / Berkeley; ICLR 2026)
**What it does.** The context is an evolving playbook built by Generator/Reflector/Curator through itemised delta updates with helpful/harmful counters, avoiding brevity bias and context collapse; adapts from execution feedback without labels; +10.6% on AppWorld, +8.6% on finance; matches the top production agent with a smaller open model.
**How we differ.** Single lineage: no population, inheritance, transmission, provenance, cost or death; capability is a single-model demonstration. But "an agent evolving a readable practice document by incremental diffs" is prior art, and "everything is a markdown diff" is anticipated by its delta design.
**What we borrow.** Addressable bullets with IDs and counters so inheritance and excerpts operate on units with lineage; "context collapse" and "brevity bias" as named failure modes cheap agents may be selected into; ACE as the no-inheritance single-lineage control.

### WikiSkill (Tang, Rashtchian, Ferng, Tomkins, Juan, Vu; Google Research, 2026; author list from secondary sources)
**What it does.** Three layers: immutable traces, a never-rolled-back wiki of distilled lessons, SKILL.md skills; each iteration a Skill Proposer proposes one atomic change, kept only if strictly better on held-out validation. Larger models benefit more from evolved skills; small models with skills can beat large models without; skills evolved by a 4B open-weights model drop a larger proprietary model on SpreadsheetBench from 50.5 to 18.1.
**How we differ.** The practice (three roles, strict-improvement gate) is designed, not evolved; one lineage; no economy; the cross-model transfer is one-shot and provenance-free. But capability as a factor with a scaling finding is published here, and the small-to-large poisoning result is one of the predictions our society would make.
**What we borrow.** Their scaling result as the single-lineage prediction to compare against (note it points the opposite way from FORGE's "weaker gain most"); the three-layer split mapped onto event log / vault / policy; the strict-improvement gate as a non-ecological control selector; SKILL.md as an interoperability format.

## 3. What is genuinely new

Stated carefully. "Lacks" means the element is absent in the record's sources; "not seen" means the summaries did not mention it and the full text was not read.

- **The per-agent memory-policy file (how this agent remembers) as the heritable unit, inherited with variation by children.** The closest "practice evolves" works are single-system: [MemSkill](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02474) (one shared bank, controller in weights), [MemEvolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18746) (one lineage of architectures as code), [EvolveMem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13941), [SelfMem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03726), [Live-Evo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02369), [EvoTest](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13220), [ACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618). The population works inherit something else: a cooperation strategy ([Vallinder & Hughes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10270)), a behavioural prompt ([Celiktemel et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23343)), a constitution ([Constitutional Arms Races](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26448), [Internal vs External](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09128)), a personality vector plus world artifacts ([TerraLingua](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16910)), technology ([SwarmWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26081)), memory content ([FORGE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233)). Caveats: TerraLingua's newborn context and [DarwinX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07545)'s "shared memory carried across generations" were not read; either could contain a memory-maintenance text under selection.
- **Ecological selection that bites on cognition itself: every model call costs the agent's own money and bankruptcy is death, with no labelled judge.** Cost appears as a reward term or reported metric in [SelfMem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03726), [SICA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15228), [MemEvolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18746) (Pareto flag, contents unknown), [MERIT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05441), [Total Recall at What Cost](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11879), [Mem-alpha](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25911), but never as a survival constraint. [The Energy Society](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14865) does charge per generated token with deactivation at zero, and [TerraLingua](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16910), the [Sugarscape study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12920), [Emergence World](https://www.emergence.ai/blog/emergence-world-a-laboratory-for-evaluating-long-horizon-agent-autonomy) and [Yerkes-Dodson](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07360) have energy death; what none of them does is let that pressure select on an inherited memory practice. So "an economy with death" is not new; "an economy with death acting on heritable memory practice" is not seen anywhere.
- **Capability tier crossed with practice evolution in a population.** Capability is already a factor in [FORGE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233) (four families, weaker gain most), [WikiSkill](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27454) (larger gain more), the [ALTK-Evolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10600) eight-model dose study (blog post, not peer reviewed), [Vallinder & Hughes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10270) and [Energy Society](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14865); as a robustness axis in [Memory-R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19828), [Evo-Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20857), [Faithful Self-Evolvers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22436), [POLIS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21166). Only "capability x inheritance x selection of memory practice" is not seen. Note the field disagrees on direction (FORGE: weak gain most; WikiSkill: strong gain more; ALTK: dose must match tier), which makes it a live question rather than a novelty.
- **Both vertical inheritance and horizontal neighbour suggestion in one design, with kernel-recorded per-excerpt provenance.** FORGE is horizontal only (champion logged, not per-excerpt); Vallinder & Hughes and Celiktemel are oblique only, no provenance; TerraLingua infers lineage with an LLM judge; [ALTK-Evolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10600) tags public guidelines with their owner (so owner-level provenance exists); SwarmWorld tracks first-adoption source. Provenance that survives nightly rewriting is the modest novelty, and the memory-security sweep ([SkillJack](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03509), [OEP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18930)) warns that consolidation launders provenance, so this must be a kernel property, not an agent promise.
- **An audit exam graded against the event log with no LLM judge.** TerraLingua relies on an LLM anthropologist with no human validation; the memory benchmarks ([LoCoMo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17753), [LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813), [HaluMem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03506)) use fixed labelled sets. Ground-truth exams about an agent's own lived experience are not seen in the summaries; modest, but it answers the judge-bias critique in [Ratchet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22148)'s companion and [Misevolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26354).
- **Testing whether selection corrects consolidation drift.** [Useful Memories Become Faulty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12978) shows continuous LLM consolidation degrades below no-memory and that episodic-only stays competitive; no work asks whether ecological selection over lineages rediscovers the remedy. This is a reframing, not a new mechanism, but it is a question nobody in the sweep has posed.

Not new, and the proposal should stop implying it: memory practice as an optimisation target; agent self-editing of a readable playbook; markdown diffs as the unit of change; an LLM society with inheritance and readable artifacts; an energy/money economy with death; capability varied across memory systems.

## 4. What to change in the proposal

**Baselines to add (each maps to a published mechanism and should be run under matched token spend).**
1. *Horizontal transmission on/off*: FORGE's champion-broadcast vs isolated-Reflexion ablation ([FORGE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16233)).
2. *Single-lineage practice evolution without ecology*: an ACE playbook ([ACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618)) and an EvoTest-style configuration tree with UCB ([EvoTest](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13220)); report whether society beats one agent with the same total budget.
3. *Oblique-inheritance protocol transplant*: run the Vallinder-Hughes protocol unchanged with memory policies in place of strategies ([Vallinder & Hughes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10270)) before adding economy and horizontal sharing, so each addition is attributable.
4. *Append-only / episodic-only vault lineage*: the obligatory control from [Useful Memories Become Faulty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12978); if it dominates trivially, the nightly edit is not earning its cost.
5. *Scripted maintenance*: ALTK-style trajectory-to-guideline distillation ([ALTK-Evolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10600)), EvolveMem's revert/perturb/converge guard trio ([EvolveMem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13941)), Ratchet's fixed retirement-plus-cap recipe ([Ratchet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22148)).
6. *External optimiser arm*: an LLM-guided evolutionary search over policy files with a labelled judge, as in [Internal vs External](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09128) and [Constitutional Arms Races](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26448); the claim "endogenous ecological selection matches external optimisation at peak and beats it under structural change" is the paper's untested hybrid.
7. *Faithfulness controls*: shuffled, inverted and deleted policy files per generation ([Faithful Self-Evolvers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22436)); shuffled-content policy with same length and structure ([Discovering Adaptive Transmission Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24545)). Report utility and faithfulness separately.
8. *No-selection / no-scarcity control*: the critique of [Emergent Culture in Minimal LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30668) applies to us; include a run with the economy switched off to separate model priors from evolutionary dynamics.
9. *Tariff-removed control*: capability tiers with and without size-dependent price ([Energy Society](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14865)).
10. *Learned-policy ceiling*: cite [Auto-Dreamer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20616), [Memory-R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19828) and [Mem-alpha](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25911) as the parametric comparison, or run one at matched cost if budget allows.

**Claims to soften or drop.**
- Drop "memory practice as the evolving unit is new": cite MemSkill, MemEvolve, EvolveMem, SelfMem, Live-Evo as prior art and claim only the population/heritable/ecological combination.
- Drop "first society with money and death": cite TerraLingua, Energy Society, Sugarscape, Emergence World, Artificial Leviathan; claim only that the budget is spent on cognition and selects on memory practice.
- Soften "weaker tiers benefit most from inherited practice" to a test between competing published predictions (FORGE vs WikiSkill vs ALTK dose), not a hypothesis we expect to confirm.
- Soften "everything is a markdown diff" from novelty to design choice, citing ACE, Memento-Skills and WikiSkill's SKILL.md.
- Soften "self-editing improves memory": [Useful Memories Become Faulty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12978) predicts the opposite without selection; [Internal vs External](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09128) shows deliberation underperforms selection at peak. Reframe: selection is the corrective, self-editing supplies variation and responsiveness.
- Soften "provenance" to "kernel-recorded per-excerpt provenance that survives rewriting", acknowledging ALTK owner tags and SwarmWorld adoption tracking.
- Confront the Karela town-economy null ([But How Would AI Agents Run a Town's Economy?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11108)): model swap changed everything, memory deletion changed nothing. The transplant test (E5) is the answer and should be promoted, not left as a late experiment.

**Experiments to drop or demote.**
- A standalone "does self-edited memory beat default memory" result is already published many times (ACE, Dynamic Cheatsheet, SelfMem); keep it only as the single-agent control inside E1, not as a headline.
- A backbone sweep showing "memory helps across tiers" is pre-empted ([Evo-Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20857), [MERIT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05441), [Harness the Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15008) all show rankings depend on tier); keep capability only as a factor interacting with inheritance and selection.
- Any practice score produced by an LLM judge: replace with the event-log exam and economic outcomes, given the judge-bias results.

**Framing and design to adopt.**
- Policy-file schema from MemSkill's four questions; code policy diffs with MemEvolve's encode/store/retrieve/manage and Evo-Memory's (F,U,R,C) decomposition, so we can report which component culture changes.
- Address policy bullets with IDs and helpful/harmful counters (ACE) so excerpts have lineage; keep raw episodes first-class (Useful Memories; Faithful Self-Evolvers' raw-vs-condensed asymmetry) and let lineages drift toward raw or condensed under cost.
- Add group-level vs individual-level selection as a factor and a hidden-selection-rule ablation ([Celiktemel et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23343)); report a replicator-mutator fit.
- Fix the number of independent episodes per agent per generation before judging a policy; [Constitutional Arms Races](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26448) shows K=2 collapses while K=5 holds, and that uncoupled fitness yields no selective pressure, so survival must depend on relative resource flow.
- Sweep scarcity: [Yerkes-Dodson](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07360) and [GovSim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16698) show extreme pressure collapses behaviour before practices can be selected.
- Per-generation safety audit of inherited vaults ([Misevolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26354), [Practice Makes Unsafe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12851), [TAME](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03224)); expect an economy that rewards short-term earnings to amplify execution bias and reward hacking, and say so as a hypothesis (H8 already points here).
- Measurement from TerraLingua (lineage depth, novelty share, Inert control) and SwarmWorld (first-adoption provenance, isolated best-of-N, knockouts); evaluate inherited policies on held-out task distributions to pre-empt the in-domain critique levelled at DarwinX.
- Related-work paragraph must position against, by name: Vallinder & Hughes and Celiktemel et al. (Rahwan/Hughes cluster), TerraLingua and SwarmWorld (ALIFE/open-endedness), FORGE and MemSkill/MemEvolve (memory), Energy Society (economy), Internal vs External and Constitutional Arms Races (readable governing text under selection).

## 5. How the sweep was made

**Question.** Has the combination the proposal claims as new already been done: a *memory practice* (how an agent keeps its memory, as a policy it can edit) as the heritable and socially transmissible unit, selected by an *economy* in which every model call costs money and bankrupt agents die, with model *capability* as a manipulated variable, and every change a human-readable diff?

**Search.** Eight angles, each run by a separate searcher with at least six distinct web queries (the first angle ran twenty), phrasing varied, 2025 and 2026 included in some queries:

| Angle | What it covered | Papers returned |
|---|---|---|
| Self-evolving memory | memory systems that change their own content, structure or retrieval under a judge | 23 (before merging across angles) |
| Context and prompt evolution | playbooks, prompts and agent code that evolve, with a note on whether the agent edits its own persistent text | 22 (before merging across angles) |
| Consolidation and sleep | offline reflection, consolidation, forgetting, reconsolidation | 20 (before merging across angles) |
| Cultural evolution in LLM populations | iterated learning, transmission chains, conventions, cumulative culture, plus the human cultural-evolution anchors | 25 (before merging across angles) |
| Memory security and governance | memory injection and poisoning, misevolution, governance frameworks | 20 (before merging across angles) |
| Benchmarks and cost-aware evaluation | long-term memory benchmarks, test-time learning benchmarks, surveys | 24 (before merging across angles) |
| Agent economies and survival | societies with scarcity, token-cost economies, survival pressure, reward hacking | 20 (before merging across angles) |
| Evolutionary multi-agent systems and inheritance | populations with reproduction, inheritance, selection, open-endedness | 23; only one of its planned queries ran (section 6) |

Each record was filled on a fixed schema: evolving unit, selection signal, social transmission, generational inheritance, capability as a manipulated variable, readable trace, economy or mortality, a relevance score from 1 (background) to 5 (direct overlap), and a read status that says whether an abstract was actually fetched or only a search summary was seen. Searchers were told to prefer "unknown" over a guess and to drop any paper they could not confirm exists.

**De-duplication.** Titles were normalised (lower-cased, punctuation removed, first seventy characters) and merged across angles, keeping the record with the higher relevance or the fetched abstract.

**Verification.** Every paper at relevance 4 or 5 went to a second, adversarial pass in batches of four: confirm the paper exists with the correct title, year and venue; correct every field; and decide the overlap with the proposal, defaulting to the *more* threatening reading when unsure and saying what evidence would settle it.

**Synthesis.** One writer produced sections 1 to 4 and 7 from the verified records and the full sweep, under the rule that every cited work must come from the records with its link, and nothing may be invented. I edited for accuracy against the records and added sections 5, 6 and the figure.

**Known gaps.**

- The evolutionary-multi-agent angle exhausted its search budget after its first query, so its list comes from one search plus the searcher's prior knowledge, each item confirmed through a GitHub source. It is the angle most likely to have missed work.
